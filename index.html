Method & known limits
  • People, not records. Cohorts count distinct people, keyed on email within each client's Healthie orgs and anchored on their earliest record. Embody: 224,698 records → 165,364 people (1.36x). Medvi: 706,236 → 703,253 (1.00x).
  • Email is undercounted, unevenly. The ramp can only see email tickets carrying a Healthie Patient ID — 87% for Embody, 81% for Medvi. The rest have Contacts.PatientID unset in Zoho Desk (the join is sound; the link was never made). Email and omni therefore read low, and by different amounts per client. Figures are not grossed up.
  • Phone is fully linked — 100%% of the sampled Embody calls matched a Healthie patient, so the phone ramp needs no coverage caveat.
  • Tenure is Healthie record age. Anchor is created_date. Embody launched ~Jun 24 2026, so its ramp beyond Day 14 is unmeasurable today rather than low.
  • Different panel maturity is the point, and the trap. Medvi's panel spans 180d+ while every Embody patient is under 6 weeks old. The ramp normalises for that by comparing equal tenures; the per-week figures above do not.
  • Clock mismatch. Call times are CST; ticket times use Zoho's Created Time, whose zone is ambiguous (three variants exist on the table). Effect is bounded to a few hours at a day boundary — immaterial for cumulative windows, not zero.
  • Archived Healthie patients are included in cohorts (Embody 70, Medvi 195 — immaterial at this scale).
