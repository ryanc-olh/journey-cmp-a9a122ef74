Method & known limits
  • All channels, one number. Voice, chat and email are combined everywhere; the only channel split on the page is the single mix line at the top, kept so chat's weight is visible.
  • Chat linkage. Connect chat rows carry no patient_id, customer_email or Org Name, and are initiation_method=API. They are linked through the contact's attributes.phone to healthie_users.phone_clean — 99.6% of Medvi chats match. That join fans out where duplicate Healthie records share a phone, so a phone→one-person map is applied first; a shared household phone therefore lands on a single person.
  • People, not records — cohorts key on email within each client's Healthie orgs, anchored on the earliest record. Embody holds ~1.36 records per person; Medvi ~1.00.
  • Email is undercounted, unevenly — only tickets carrying a Healthie Patient ID are visible (87% Embody, 81% Medvi). Not grossed up. Voice is ~100% linked; chat 99.6%.
  • The ramp is not window-scoped. It uses each person's full contact history against their tenure, so it is identical across both views. Only the window-scoped sections change.
  • Tenure is Healthie record age. Embody launched ~2026-06-24, so past Day 14 its ramp is unmeasurable rather than low.
  • Ticket times use Zoho's Created Time while calls/chats use CST — bounded to a few hours at a day boundary. Archived Healthie patients are still counted (immaterial at this scale).
